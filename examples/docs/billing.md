# Billing

Orbit Notes is billed per workspace. The Team plan costs 8 dollars per member per month, and the annual option gives two months free.

## Invoices

Invoices are issued on the first day of each month and emailed to every workspace owner. Past invoices can be downloaded from Settings, under the Billing tab.

## Refunds

Refunds are available within 14 days of a charge. To request one, write to billing@orbitnotes.example.com with the invoice number.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails, Orbit Notes retries it three times over seven days. After the last failed attempt the workspace becomes read-only until the balance is paid.
